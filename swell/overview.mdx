Swell is SendWhale's built-in AI campaign assistant. It works directly inside your selected workspace, drawing on your brand settings, references, images, memories, and integrations to help you create campaigns that feel on-brand from the first draft. Everything Swell knows is scoped to the workspace you have open — switch workspaces and Swell immediately works within that brand's context instead.

## What Swell can do

Swell is designed to handle the full creative and operational loop of email marketing. Within a conversation, you can ask Swell to:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Draft & refine emails" icon="pen-nib">
    Write email campaigns from a brief, then refine copy, layout, tone, subject lines, and calls to action through natural conversation.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Read your website" icon="globe">
    Supply a public URL and Swell will fetch and analyze your site's content to inform campaign messaging and extract relevant brand details.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Work with your image library" icon="image">
    Select images from your workspace library to include in drafts, or attach images directly to a conversation for visual direction.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Create campaigns & audiences" icon="bullhorn">
    Turn a finished draft into a campaign and build or select the audience that should receive it — without leaving the conversation.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Save brand facts" icon="bookmark">
    Ask Swell to remember key brand details — voice, product names, preferred phrasing — so future drafts stay consistent.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Apply design references" icon="swatchbook">
    Reference saved design styles to guide the visual direction of new campaigns.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Workspace context

Swell's quality depends directly on the richness of your workspace context. The more complete your brand settings, references, memories, and integrations are, the more accurately Swell will reflect your brand in every draft. Keep this information current — especially after rebrands, product updates, or team changes.

<Note>
  Swell's available capabilities depend on your account access level, workspace role, and whether AI provider credentials have been configured for your account. Some features may show as unavailable if a required provider or permission is missing.
</Note>
