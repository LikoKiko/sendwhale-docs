SendWhale lets you create public booking pages so contacts and visitors can schedule calls and meetings with you directly — no back-and-forth email required. Each booking page is powered by an availability configuration you define in your workspace.

<Note>
  Meeting scheduling (`/app/scheduler`, `/book/:slug`) is completely separate from campaign scheduling (the campaign calendar). They serve different purposes — one manages meeting bookings, the other manages when marketing emails send.
</Note>

## Setting your availability

Open **Scheduler** from your workspace sidebar and create a new availability configuration. Define:

- **Available hours** — the days of the week and the time windows when you can be booked
- **Buffer time** — the gap to leave before and after each meeting so bookings do not stack back-to-back

Save the configuration to generate a public booking page.

## Your public booking page

Each availability configuration produces a unique URL at `/book/:slug`. Visitors who open this URL can see your available time slots and pick one that works for them. They enter their name and email to confirm the booking.

## Sharing your booking link

Include your `/book/:slug` link wherever your contacts are most likely to see it:

- As a call-to-action button in your email campaigns
- On your website's contact or about page
- In your email signature

## Managing bookings

View all upcoming and past bookings from the **Scheduler** section of your workspace at `/app/scheduler`. Use this view to monitor what is on your calendar, review past meeting history, and identify any bookings that need follow-up.
