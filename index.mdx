SendWhale is an email marketing platform built around Swell, your AI campaign assistant. From brand setup and audience management to campaign drafting, scheduling, and analytics — everything lives in your workspace so you can move from idea to inbox with confidence.

<Note>
  SendWhale is currently in **private beta**. The public homepage at [gosendwhale.com](https://www.gosendwhale.com) is a waitlist. Registration is available at [/signup](https://www.gosendwhale.com/signup). Some features described here require additional account or provider setup — look for the **Availability** callout on each page.
</Note>

## Make your first campaign

The fastest way to get started is to set up your brand workspace, build a consented audience, draft an email with Swell, and send it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Register at [gosendwhale.com/signup](https://www.gosendwhale.com/signup), confirm your email, and complete the onboarding flow to set up your first workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your brand">
    Add your brand name, colors, logo, voice, and website to your workspace so Swell can draft emails that sound like you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import your contacts">
    Upload a CSV or spreadsheet of subscribers, map fields, and assign them to an audience. Importing an address does not create marketing consent — use signup forms or record consent manually.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Draft and send">
    Open Swell, describe the campaign you want, review the draft, run preflight checks, send a test, and confirm delivery to your audience.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key concepts

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Workspace** | Your brand's isolated environment — all campaigns, contacts, and settings live inside a workspace. |
| **Swell** | SendWhale's AI campaign assistant, scoped to your workspace's brand data. |
| **Audience** | A named list of contacts you send campaigns to. |
| **Campaign** | An email you draft, review, and deliver to an audience. |
| **Template** | A reusable email design you can base campaigns on. |
| **Automation** | A workflow that sends emails automatically based on triggers and conditions. |
| **Hosted Page** | A public newsletter or landing page at `/n/:slug`. |
| **Integration** | A third-party service connected to your workspace (CRM, e-commerce, chat, etc.). |

## Beta availability

Features in SendWhale are in one of three states:

- ✅ **Available** — Implemented and ready to use with a standard account.
- ⚙️ **Requires setup** — Implemented, but needs additional account or provider configuration before it works.
- 🔜 **Planned / unavailable** — Not yet ready for general use.

Each feature page includes an **Availability** callout when setup is required.
