Automations let you send emails automatically based on contact behavior or conditions — without manually scheduling each message. You design a workflow once in the visual builder, then SendWhale handles enrollment and execution as contacts match your trigger criteria.

## How automations work

The **workflow builder** is a visual editor where you design the full shape of an automation flow. You place triggers, conditions, delays, and actions on a canvas, then connect them into a sequence SendWhale follows for every enrolled contact.

### Triggers

A trigger is the event that starts a contact's journey through the workflow. Supported triggers include:

- A contact joins an audience
- A contact updates a field
- A date-based trigger (e.g., a contact's anniversary date)
- Other supported events — open the builder to see all available triggers for your plan

### Actions

Actions are the steps SendWhale performs for each enrolled contact. Supported actions include:

- Send an email
- Wait a delay period
- Apply a tag to a contact
- Update a contact field
- Other supported actions — check the builder for the full list

### Conditions

Add conditions to branch your workflow based on contact attributes or previous behavior. A condition evaluates to true or false and routes contacts down different paths, letting you send different messages to different segments within the same automation.

### Contact enrollment

Contacts enter a workflow when they match the trigger condition. Each contact follows the workflow steps in order, respecting any delays or branching conditions you have configured.

<Info>
  Automation capabilities depend on your SendWhale plan. Check the builder for which triggers and actions are available on your plan.
</Info>
