Brand settings define the identity that SendWhale and Swell use when building and drafting campaigns for your workspace. Every field here — from your logo to your footer address — feeds directly into how your emails look and what Swell writes. Brand settings are per-workspace, so each workspace you manage can have a completely independent brand.

## Brand settings fields

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Core identity">
    - **Name** — your business or brand name as it should appear in emails and hosted pages
    - **Website** — your primary website URL; used when importing brand source material
    - **Description** — a short summary of what your business does; Swell draws on this when framing email copy
    - **Logo** — your brand logo, displayed in email templates and hosted pages
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Visual style">
    - **Colors** — your primary and secondary brand colors, applied to email templates
    - **Fonts** — the typefaces associated with your brand; used in template styling
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Brand voice">
    Set a brand voice to guide Swell's tone when drafting email copy. For example: *professional and concise*, *warm and conversational*, or *bold and direct*. Swell applies this voice to every draft it generates for this workspace.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Social links">
    Add links to your business's social media profiles. These can be included in email footers and hosted pages.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Sitemap and navigation">
    Provide your site's navigation structure or key page URLs. Swell can reference these when creating links or suggesting content relevant to your audience.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Legal and footer facts">
    Store the facts that appear in every campaign email footer: your business address, company legal name, and any other required disclosures. These values are pre-filled from onboarding and can be updated here at any time.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Importing brand settings from your website

Supply your website URL in the brand settings form and select **Import from website**. SendWhale fetches publicly available content from your site to pre-populate fields like your description, colors, social links, and footer facts.

<Warning>
  Never rely on auto-imported data for legal disclaimers, prices, compliance text, or your business address. Always review and manually verify everything that was imported before saving — automated imports can be incomplete, outdated, or inaccurate.
</Warning>

After importing, go through each field and confirm the values are correct. Pay particular attention to your footer facts and any legal statements.

## Saving and switching workspaces

Brand settings are saved per workspace. When you switch to a different workspace, SendWhale loads that workspace's brand settings automatically. Changes you make in one workspace do not affect any other workspace.

<Note>
  If your business address changes, update it in brand settings as soon as possible. Your footer address appears in every campaign email and must remain accurate for CAN-SPAM and GDPR compliance.
</Note>
