The campaign editor is where you build and polish your email before it reaches your audience. You can construct layouts visually using drag-and-drop content blocks, switch to raw HTML for advanced customization, preview your design on different devices, and access previous revisions if you need to roll back a change.

## Content blocks

Drag blocks from the panel on the left into your email canvas. Available block types include:

- **Text** — paragraphs, headings, and inline formatting
- **Image** — insert from your media library or upload directly
- **Button** — a styled call-to-action linked to any URL
- **Social links** — icons linked to your social media profiles
- **Divider** — a horizontal rule to separate sections
- **Spacer** — adjustable vertical whitespace
- **Columns** — multi-column layout containers for side-by-side content

## Layout controls

Select any block or section to reveal its layout settings in the right-hand panel:

- **Column structure** — choose single-column or multi-column layouts
- **Padding** — adjust inner spacing on blocks and sections
- **Background colors** — set section and block backgrounds independently
- **Font settings** — choose font family, size, weight, and color for text blocks

## Images

To add an image, drag an Image block onto the canvas and either:

- **Insert from library** — pick from images you have already uploaded to your media library
- **Upload inline** — upload a new image directly from your device

Always add meaningful alt text to every image so your email remains accessible and readable when images are blocked.

## Buttons

A Button block links a call-to-action label to any URL. Set the button text, destination URL, and styling (color, border radius, padding) from the block settings panel.

## Social links

The Social links block lets you add icon links to your brand's social profiles. Configure each platform's URL in the block settings.

## Footer

Every email sent through SendWhale automatically includes an unsubscribe link and your business address in the footer. These are required by law in most jurisdictions and cannot be removed.

## Device preview

Use the device preview toggle in the editor toolbar to switch between **Desktop** and **Mobile** views. Review your layout at both sizes before sending — column-heavy layouts often need adjustments to render well on small screens.

## HTML / code view

Switch to the HTML view from the editor toolbar to edit the raw email HTML directly. Use this for advanced customizations that are not possible with the drag-and-drop blocks. Changes made in code view are reflected in the visual editor, though complex custom HTML may not be fully editable as individual blocks.

## Autosave and revisions

The editor autosaves your changes as you work. To access previous versions:

1. Open the editor toolbar
2. Select **Revisions**
3. Browse the revision history and restore any earlier version

<Warning>
Restoring a revision replaces your current unsaved changes. Make sure you do not need your current edits before restoring an older version.
</Warning>

## Concurrent edit conflicts

If a teammate saves a new version of the campaign while you are editing, SendWhale displays a conflict notice. Refresh the editor to load their latest version before continuing your edits — otherwise your save may overwrite their changes.

<Note>
The campaign editor and the hosted-page editor are separate tools with different document models. Edits you make to a campaign in the campaign editor do not affect any hosted pages, and vice versa.
</Note>
